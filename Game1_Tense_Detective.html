🔍 Tense Detective

Learning objective: choose the right tense – present simple (facts, habits), simple past (finished, time given), present perfect (no time given / up to now).
Level: 1st year secondary (A2). Skill: grammar in context (reading). Teacher tip: play on the board after Task 8; ask “Why?” after each answer, using the clue words. Learners often say “I have seen him yesterday” – stress that a finished time word means simple past.
